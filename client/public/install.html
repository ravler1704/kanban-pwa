Install Offline Kanban

A Kanban board that works offline and syncs across your devices.

Open in browser
App installed — find the Kanban icon among your apps.

Android

Google Play soon Download APK

APK: open the downloaded file and allow installation from this source if asked. Or without a file: Chrome → menu ⋮ → “Install app”.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open the site in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (square with an arrow).
  3. Choose “Add to Home Screen” and tap “Add”.

The icon will appear on your Home Screen; reminder notifications work only in the app installed this way (iOS 16.4+).

Windows, macOS, Linux

  • Chrome, Edge, Yandex Browser: install icon on the right of the address bar or menu → “Install Offline Kanban”.
  • Safari on Mac (macOS 14+): “File” → “Add to Dock”.
  • Firefox: use the site in a tab — Firefox does not support app installation.

One app on all your devices

Data is stored on the device, works offline, and syncs through your account.
