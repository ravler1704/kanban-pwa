Features

Everything you need so things don't get lost

Simple like sticky notes on a fridge, and powerful enough for renovation, moving, studying, or a small team.

Works without internet

All boards are stored on the device, so the app opens instantly — on a plane, in the subway, in the countryside. Changes are queued and sent when connection returns. If two people edit the same thing, the app shows both versions and lets you choose.

Sync across devices

Start on desktop — continue on phone. Automatically, no save button.

Folders and boards of any depth

Home, work, projects — organize as you like. Search across all boards and filter by tags and people.

Telegram-style chats

Chat for each board and card, direct messages. Replies, quotes, edit and delete, images, delivered and read checkmarks.

Reminders with notifications

Unlimited reminders per card, with repeats. Push notification arrives even if the app is closed.

Sharing

Invite family or colleagues by email — to specific folders. Or share a public link visible without sign-in.

Board, list, calendar and Gantt

Classic Kanban board, table, calendar with deadlines, or Gantt chart — one click.

Light and dark theme

Follows system, board backgrounds, comfortable mobile UI.

Export to Excel and PDF

A board, multiple boards, or the whole space — to .xlsx or printable PDF. Works offline too.

Free and open source

No subscriptions or ads. All code is on GitHub: you can inspect or run your own server.

Board templates

Ready-made columns — one click into your space

Choose a template, sign in if needed — and a new board with columns and background will appear in “My space”. You can publish your own templates in the app.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscriptions, trial periods, or ads. Source code is open on GitHub.

Where is my data stored?

First of all — on your device (in browser storage), so everything works offline. A copy is stored on the server to sync devices and share boards. Only people you invited have access to a board.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, completely: you can create and move cards, check subtasks, write messages. Everything will be sent when the network returns. Internet is needed only for the first sign-in.

How to install on iPhone or iPad?

  1. Open the site in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (square with arrow).
  3. Choose “Add to Home Screen” → “Add”.
Reminder notifications work in the app installed this way (iOS 16.4+).

What about Android and desktop?

On Android — download APK or in Chrome choose “Install app”; coming soon to Google Play. On Mac — in Safari “File” → “Add to Dock”. On Windows, macOS and Linux — install icon in Chrome or Edge address bar. Details on the installation page.

Can I work with family or a team?

Yes: invite people by email to specific folders, discuss tasks in board and card chats, assign people.

Try it right now

It takes less than a minute. And if you like it — install on all your devices.
